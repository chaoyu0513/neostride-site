Privacy choices

You control your data.

Manage Apple Health, coaching history, Apple Watch schedules, and complete account deletion.

Apple Health permission

You can review, change, or revoke Neo Stride's Apple Health read permission at any time in Apple Health or iOS Settings. Source Apple Health data remains managed by Apple Health.

Coaching-history export and deletion

After signing in to Neo Stride, you can export coaching history as JSON or delete coaching history from Profile. This scope includes plan versions, coaching decisions, plan adjustments, structured post-run feedback, confirmed session outcomes, and derived kilometre splits. Deletion cannot be undone.

Coaching-history deletion does not delete the account, sign-in data, current plan, source Apple Health data, or Apple Watch schedules.

Complete account deletion

Email chaoyu0513@gmail.com from the email used for your account so the requester can be verified. After verification, we delete the sign-in account and account-owned service data within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for other data-rights request periods.

Apple Watch schedules

You can cancel a confirmed Apple Watch schedule; cancellation does not mark the workout complete.

Website data

This static site uses no analytics, tracking, advertising cookies, or third-party embeds.
