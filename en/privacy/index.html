Privacy

The privacy policy is under review.

Before public beta, Neo Stride will complete its formal privacy policy, support contact details, and App Store privacy disclosures.

This is a pre-review status page, not a formal privacy policy. It must not be used as an App Store or TestFlight privacy URL.

Current product principles

  • Neo Stride reads running-training and recovery data only after the runner grants Apple Health permission.
  • It does not read routes or location data, and it never represents a scheduled workout as a completed run.
  • Coaching data can be exported or deleted in the app.
  • This site has no analytics, tracking, advertising cookies, or third-party embeds.

Before publication

The final policy will name the data controller and contact, include an effective date, explain data processing and retention, describe Apple Health data, and explain privacy rights and choices.
