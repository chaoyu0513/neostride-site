Support

Neo Stride Beta Support

The first wave is limited to invited runners aged 18 or older. Invitations are private by email; there is no public TestFlight link.

Contact support

James Chen owns support responses. Email chaoyu0513@gmail.com.

What to include

A useful report includes the app version, iOS version, whether Apple Health or Apple Watch was involved, reproduction steps, the expected result, the actual result, and an optional screenshot with private information removed.

Do not send OTPs, access tokens, Supabase keys or URLs, Apple account identifiers, raw Apple Health samples, GPS routes, or another person's information.

Safety and privacy issues

Report safety or privacy problems, including any case that shows another person's data, immediately. Neo Stride is not an emergency or medical service and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or injury prediction.

Data rights and complete account deletion

Send the request from the email used for your account to chaoyu0513@gmail.com. We decide access or copy requests within 15 days and supplementation, correction, cessation, or deletion requests within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy and Privacy choices for complete details.
